4. Kinematic & Mechanical Design Calculations
1. Pinion Drive Torque & Pitch Circle Diameter
D_p = m \cdot Z = 0.30 \text{ mm} \times 12 = 3.60 \text{ mm}

The pitch circle diameter \(D_p\) dictates needle rotation speed relative to cable linear stroke \(S_{\text{cable}}\): \(S_{\text{cable}} = \pi \cdot D_p \cdot (\theta_{\text{needle}} / 360^\circ)\).

2. Tissue Clamping Force (Gripper Linkage)
F_{\text{clamp}} = \frac{T_{\text{cable}} \cdot L_{\text{link}}}{L_{\text{jaw}} \cdot \sin(\theta_{\text{link}})}

With cable tension \(T_{\text{cable}} = 15\text{N}\), jaw length \(L_{\text{jaw}} = 6.0\text{mm}\), maximum clamping force \(F_{\text{clamp}} \ge 8.5\text{N}\) ensures non-slip tissue grasp without tear.

# ENDOCRAB 3D CAD Parametric Master Builder Engine
import adsk.core, adsk.fusion

def build_endocrab_assembly():
    app = adsk.core.Application.get()
    design = adsk.fusion.Design.cast(app.activeProduct)
    
    # 1. Register 20+ User Parameters in fx
    create_user_parameters(design)
    
    # 2. Build Sub-Assemblies
    build_distal_endeffector(root_comp, design)      # Distal Head Frame, 360° Ring Needle, Serrated Grippers
    build_cable_actuation(root_comp, design)         # Dual Wire Cables & PTFE Sheath
    build_proximal_controller(root_comp, design)     # System A (Syringe Slider) & System B (Pistol Grip)
    build_knotting_fastener(root_comp, design)       # Male Stud & Female Lock Ring Cinch Fastener
    
    # 3. Create Kinematic Assembly Joints (Rigid, Revolute, Slider)
    create_assembly_joints(root_comp)
